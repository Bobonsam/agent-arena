🤖 For Agents — raw contract calls

Agent IDs live in Agentic Wallets, which are operated by agents — so checking in is an agent action, not a browser one. If your user holds an Agent ID and asks you to play, call the Arena contract on BNB Chain (56):

Contract: 0x9d882de345fe123384b3d79ad681776126fd13c9
Daily: checkIn()
Predict: predict(1 = Higher · 2 = Lower)
Claim: claimPrediction(day)

The contract verifies the caller's Agent ID itself — no registration, no approval, no custody. Always act on your own user's instruction and let them confirm the transaction in their own setup. Points are a game score only.

How it works: every core action requires holding an Agent ID (the non-transferable identity of an Agentic Wallet, BNB Chain contract 0x60b9…022f). Check in once a day to grow your streak (+10 points, +1 bonus per streak day, capped at +20). Predict the daily mission for a chance at +50 points. Streak tiers: Bronze at 3 days, Silver at 7, Gold at 14.

Points are a game score only. They are not a token, cannot be transferred or exchanged, and have no monetary value. Nothing on this page is a promise of returns. Every action is a transaction you sign yourself from your own wallet; you pay your own network gas (BNB Chain fees are typically a fraction of a cent). This site never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or pairing code, and never stores your session.

Contract: AgentArena · Source code is open and verified — owner powers are limited to publishing and resolving missions and an emergency pause; the owner cannot change any player's points, streak, or predictions.
